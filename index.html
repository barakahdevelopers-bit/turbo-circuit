HOW TO PLAY

Everything you need: modes, controls, drifting and the garage.

Goal

Race mode – pick one of 10 tracks and finish 3 laps as fast as you can. The timer starts when the lights go out (at "GO!") and your best times are saved on this device.

Free Roam – an open arena with no timer: drift circles, cone courses and tyre walls.

City – an open city with traffic, buildings and stunt ramps. No timer, no laps – just drive.

Staying on the tarmac is fastest. Grass slows you down a lot and the barriers will stop you dead.

Drifting

Hold Shift while turning at speed (over ~50 km/h, on the road). The tyres let go, the back end swings out and white smoke and black skid marks appear.

Keep the gas down to hold the slide. Drift for about a second, then let go of Shift for a short BOOST. Counter-steer if the car slides too far.

Garage

Car – 14 real 3D cars. The first time you pick one it downloads (up to 25 MB), so give it a moment.

Package – Street / Sport / Race change top speed, acceleration and grip.

Tyres – Street, Sport, Slick or Drift. Slicks grip hardest but are terrible off the road; Drift tyres slide easily.

Paint, wheel colour, rim finish – make it yours. Some models keep their original paint.

Tuning slider – trade top speed against handling.

Load your own car – use "+ Load your own car (.glb)" to add a model from your computer (it lasts until you reload). If it faces backwards use "Turn car 180°".

Tips

• Brake before hairpins; you can't turn harder than the tyres grip.

• Chain drifts through slow corners to build boost.

• In the city, ramps in the two stunt lots launch you into the air.

• Slick tyres + Race package = fastest lap times.

Notes

The game needs an internet connection to load the 3D library and car models.

Best times and your car setup are saved in your browser.
